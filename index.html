FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Baking Subscription Box Details

When do the exclusive baking boxes ship each month?

All monthly subscription boxes are prepared and dispatched during the third week of each month. Local pickup is available from our Norwood studio, or boxes ship across Ontario and Canada with tracked courier delivery.

What fresh wet ingredients do I need to supply at home?

Every box includes 100% of the specialty dry ingredients (chocolates, spices, flours, sugars, toppings) and professional tools. You will typically only need basic household kitchen staples such as fresh butter, eggs, or whole milk.

Can I pause, skip a month, or cancel my subscription?

Yes! Through your Shopify customer portal, you can pause, skip an upcoming box, or cancel anytime with one click prior to your monthly renewal date.

MY CAKEHOUSE

IT ALL STARTED...

I was entirely unaware how making cupcakes for a friend’s sister’s wedding (you get that?) would change my life. As time went on I got better and I grew not only with my skills but in business. I will never forget that first commission and how it gave me that little kick start and got me where I am today.

One of my favourite things to do is to cook large holiday dinners or hosting dinner parties. I have even been writing a cookbook for the last few years. Spreads that will knock your socks off and loosen your belt. Funny thing is before that first wedding I barely remember baking! Weird I know! I think I may have only started when we made cupcakes in a jar for my wedding as favours 10 years ago and I still hear about what a hit those were.

Building upon my self-education, I continue my skills by frequently taking courses both online and in person. You’re never too old to learn I say — I even take classes on things I know how to do because you can always pick up something new.
